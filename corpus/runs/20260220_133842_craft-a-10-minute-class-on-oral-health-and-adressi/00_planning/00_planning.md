# Task List

1. Generate 3–4 measurable learning objectives for a 10-minute class on oral health focusing on identifying and addressing small oral ulcers (aphthous ulcers) where the gums meet the cheek, targeted at a general adult audience
2. Map the generated learning objectives to Bloom's taxonomy levels to ensure appropriate cognitive demand for a brief introductory class
3. Draft a 2–3 module syllabus for the 10-minute oral health class aligned to the objectives (e.g., anatomy/identification, causes & prevention, home treatment of gum-line ulcers), with suggested instructional strategies
4. Create a pacing guide distributing the 10 minutes across content delivery, visual demonstration, and Q&A for the oral ulcer class
5. Design a brief formative assessment (e.g., 3–5 question quiz or checklist) with a rubric aligned to each learning objective on oral ulcer identification and care
6. Synthesize 5–6 learning resources (articles, videos, patient handouts) from .edu, .gov, and OER sources on aphthous ulcers, oral hygiene, and gum-line ulcer treatment
7. Generate a 10-slide reveal.js slide deck for the 10-minute oral health class covering ulcer identification, causes, home care, and when to seek professional help
8. Design a one-page take-home worksheet with 3–4 activities for learners on recognizing oral ulcers, proper oral hygiene steps, and a self-care action plan
