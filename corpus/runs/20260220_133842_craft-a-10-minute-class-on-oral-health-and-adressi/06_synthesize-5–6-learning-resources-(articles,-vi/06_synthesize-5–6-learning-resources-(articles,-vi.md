## Curated Learning Resources: Aphthous Ulcers, Oral Hygiene & Gum-Line Ulcer Treatment

### 1. **NIH / NIDCR — "Canker Sores (Aphthous Ulcers)"** *(Patient Handout / Article — .gov)*
- **Source:** National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research (NIDCR)
- **URL:** `https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/health-info/mouth-sores`
- **Type:** Patient education article
- **Covers:** Definition, causes, triggers, symptoms, when to see a provider, and home care for aphthous ulcers. Printable for patient handouts.

### 2. **MedlinePlus — "Canker Sores"** *(Article + Video — .gov)*
- **Source:** U.S. National Library of Medicine / MedlinePlus
- **URL:** `https://medlineplus.gov/cankersores.html`
- **Type:** Patient-facing article with embedded video and links to clinical trials
- **Covers:** Overview, diagnosis, treatment options, prevention, and links to related topics (oral hygiene, mouth diseases). Available in English and Spanish.

### 3. **CDC — "Oral Health: Hygiene"** *(Patient Education / Infographic — .gov)*
- **Source:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
- **URL:** `https://www.cdc.gov/oral-health/about/index.html`
- **Type:** Educational webpage with downloadable resources
- **Covers:** Oral hygiene fundamentals, brushing/flossing guidelines, gum disease prevention, and links to state oral health programs.

### 4. **University of Michigan — Oral Pathology Teaching Resources** *(OER / .edu)*
- **Source:** University of Michigan School of Dentistry
- **URL:** `https://pathology.med.umich.edu/oral-path`
- **Type:** Open educational images and case descriptions
- **Covers:** Clinical photographs and histopathology of aphthous ulcers, gingival lesions, and other oral mucosal conditions. Useful for visual learner modules.

### 5. **OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology — "The Mouth, Pharynx, and Esophagus"** *(OER Textbook Chapter)*
- **Source:** OpenStax (Rice University)
- **URL:** `https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-3-the-mouth-pharynx-and-esophagus`
- **Type:** Open-access textbook chapter
- **Covers:** Anatomy of the oral cavity, mucosal tissue structure, gingival anatomy — provides the foundational science context for understanding where and why ulcers form along the gum line.

### 6. **ADA (via MouthHealthy.org) — "Canker Sores"** *(Patient Handout / Article)*
- **Source:** American Dental Association
- **URL:** `https://www.mouthhealthy.org/all-topics-a-z/canker-sores`
- **Type:** Patient handout-style article
- **Covers:** Practical guidance on managing canker sores, when to seek treatment, and connection to overall oral hygiene practices. Designed for patient-friendly reading level.
