## Module 2: Causes & Prevention (3–4 minutes)

**Objective:** Learners will understand the primary causes of gum-line ulcers and adopt at least two preventive habits.

**Content:**
- Common causes: mechanical trauma (aggressive brushing, ill-fitting dental appliances), nutritional deficiencies (B12, iron, folate), stress, and immune-related triggers
- Risk factors: poor oral hygiene, acidic/spicy foods, tobacco use
- Prevention strategies: gentle brushing technique, balanced nutrition, stress management, regular dental checkups

**Instructional Strategy:**
- **Cause-and-effect matching** — brief verbal or handout-based activity linking causes to prevention strategies
- **"Two things you'll change" commitment** — ask each learner to mentally select two preventive habits to adopt
