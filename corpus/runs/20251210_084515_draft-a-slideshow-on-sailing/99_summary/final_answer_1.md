# Sailing Slideshow Curriculum Outline

## Summary
A 45-minute beginner-friendly slideshow on sailing for a general audience. Approachable, fun tone emphasizing essentials, safe practice, and adventure. Covers boat basics, wind dynamics, maneuvers, safety, and next steps.

## Learning Objectives
- Grasp core sailing concepts (boat parts, wind).
- Apply basic maneuvers (tacking, jibing, docking).
- Practice safety protocols and reflect on personal takeaways.
- Build confidence to start sailing independently.

## Module Outline
Structured as 10 scannable slides/modules with key content, visuals, and speaker notes.

| Module/Slide | Key Content | Visuals/Activities |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **1. Hook: Feel the Wind!** | Imagine sailing; sailing is accessible joy. | Boat image; audience smiles/nods. |
| **2. Purpose & Learning Objectives** | Why sail (freedom, skills); list core learnings. | Icons for objectives. |
| **3. Essential Concepts: Know Your Boat** | Hull, sail, rudder/tiller, boom. | Simple diagram; quick quiz ("What's the rudder?"). |
| **4. Essential Concepts: Wind Magic** | Apparent/true wind; points of sail (close-hauled, beam reach, run). | Arm demo for directions. |
| **5. Application: Tacking & Jibing** | Tack (upwind zigzag); jibe (downwind turn). | Act-out simulation. |
| **6. Application: Docking & Safety** | Slow approach, fenders, life jackets; weather checks. | Safety icons; quick story. |
| **7. Reflection: Your Takeaways** | Recap basics, maneuvers, safety; journal idea. | Poll/shares. |
| **8. Next Steps: Set Sail!** | Lessons, YouTube, lake practice; Q&A. | Call-to-action graphics. |

## Assessments
- **Formative (Embedded)**:
  - Quiz: Identify boat parts (Slide 3).
  - Demo: Audience mimics wind directions/tacks (Slides 4-5).
  - Poll: Share one takeaway (Slide 7).
- **Reflective**:
  - Journal prompt: "One practice idea" (Slide 7).
- **Success Criteria**: Audience nods engagement; 80% recall key terms in Q&A.

## Pacing
Total: 45 minutes. Flexible for interaction.

| Module | Time Allocation |
