## Learning Objectives

**Course:** AI ethics
**Audience:** middle school students
**Level:** Beginner

### Objectives
1. Define artificial intelligence by comparing human decision-making processes with computer algorithms using at least two real-world examples.
2. Identify potential biases in AI systems by analyzing a specific scenario (such as a social media feed or video game recommendation) to explain how it might impact different groups of people.
3. Evaluate the trade-offs between personal privacy and AI convenience by describing how a smart device collects data and proposing one method to protect user information.
4. Distinguish between authentic content and AI-generated media (such as deepfakes) by applying a verification checklist to two sample images or videos.
5. Formulate a "Code of Ethics" for a fictional AI assistant by predicting three possible negative consequences and establishing rules to prevent them.
