## Learning Objectives

**Course:** AI Ethics
**Audience:** middle school students
**Level:** Beginner

### Objectives
1. Analyze a simulated social media feed to identify at least one example of algorithmic bias, providing a written explanation of how the bias might affect different users.
2. Create a visual "data journey" map that traces how a specific piece of personal information (e.g., a photo or location) is collected by an AI system, accurately labeling three key stages of the process.
3. Evaluate a set of paired images and text samples to distinguish between human-created and AI-generated content, achieving at least 80% accuracy on the identification task.
4. Propose a "Fairness Code" consisting of three rules for a hypothetical AI chatbot used in school, ensuring each rule addresses a specific ethical concern such as honesty, privacy, or inclusivity.
5. Construct a persuasive paragraph arguing for or against the use of AI in a specific future career field, citing at least two ethical considerations discussed in the course materials.
