# AI Ethics: Being a Good Digital Citizen
**Middle School Edition**  
*Duration: 45 Minutes*

---

## Magic or Machine?

Have you ever wondered how your phone knows what song you’ll like next? It’s Artificial Intelligence (AI)! AI is like a super-fast robot brain that helps us.

But, just like people, robots need rules to play nice. Today, we are going to learn how to make sure AI is fair for everyone.

Notes: Ask students to raise their hands if they have used Siri, Alexa, or TikTok. Explain that these all use AI.

---

## What is AI Ethics?

"Ethics" is just a fancy word for knowing the difference between right and wrong.

AI Ethics is about making sure computer programs treat everyone fairly. It’s asking: "Is this machine being kind, helpful, and honest?"

Notes: Relate this to school rules. Just as we have rules to keep recess fair, we need rules for AI.

---

## The Copycat Problem (Bias)

Did you know AI learns from humans? If humans are unfair, the AI learns to be unfair too. This is called "Bias."

Imagine teaching a parrot to only say mean things. The parrot isn't evil, it just learned bad words. AI can do the same thing!

Notes: Emphasize that AI doesn't have feelings; it just copies the data we give it.

---

## Privacy Matters

AI needs lots of information to learn. It looks at your photos, chats, and locations.

But, is it okay for a computer to know everything about you? We need to protect our secrets so they aren't used in the wrong way.

Notes: Ask students why they wouldn't give their diary to a stranger. Explain that data is like a digital diary.

---

## Activity: The Blue Shirt Team

Let's practice! Imagine a robot picks the kickball team. But, the robot only picks kids wearing blue shirts.

**Discuss with a partner:**
1. Is this fair?
2. Why would the robot do this?
3. How do we fix it?

Notes: Allow 3-5 minutes for discussion. The answer is usually "bad data" or "bad instructions."

---

## Key Takeaways

Here is what we learned today:
*   **AI is Human-Made:** It learns from us.
*   **Watch for Bias:** AI can be unfair if we aren't careful.
*   **Protect Privacy:** Keep your personal data safe.

Notes: Recap these points clearly. These are the main learning objectives for the session.

---

## Be a Tech Detective

You don't have to be a computer scientist to care about ethics.

When you use technology, ask yourself: "Is this fair?" and "Is this safe?" You are the future, and you can help build a better, kinder internet!

Notes: End on an empowering note. Encourage them to be critical thinkers.
