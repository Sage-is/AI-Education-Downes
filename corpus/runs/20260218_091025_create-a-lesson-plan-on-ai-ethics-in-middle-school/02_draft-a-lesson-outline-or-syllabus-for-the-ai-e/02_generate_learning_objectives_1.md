## Learning Objectives

**Course:** AI Ethics
**Audience:** undergraduate students
**Level:** Beginner

### Objectives
1. Differentiate between ethical challenges specific to artificial intelligence and traditional software development by identifying at least three distinct areas of risk.
2. Analyze a provided case study of an algorithmic failure to pinpoint the specific stage in the data pipeline where bias was introduced.
3. Evaluate a contemporary AI application’s data collection methods against fundamental privacy principles to determine if user consent is adequately protected.
4. Critique the use of opaque "black box" models in high-stakes decision-making by proposing two specific strategies for increasing model explainability.
5. Predict the potential long-term societal impacts of AI-driven automation in a chosen industry, supporting the forecast with at least two evidence-based factors.
6. Construct a set of ethical guidelines for a hypothetical AI project that explicitly addresses issues of accountability, transparency, and fairness.
