# Worksheet: AI Ethics

**Overview**
*   **Audience:** High School Students
*   **Skill Focus:** Critical thinking and ethical reasoning
*   **Estimated Time:** 30 minutes
*   **Materials:** Pencil, Notebook

## Understanding AI Ethics

Define the core concepts of artificial intelligence and explore why moral guidelines are necessary for technology.

1.  Write a one-sentence definition of "Artificial Intelligence" using your own words, avoiding technical jargon.
2.  List three distinct ways AI systems currently help people in their daily lives.
3.  Identify one major risk or danger associated with the widespread use of AI.

## Case Study Analysis

Analyze a realistic scenario to identify the ethical conflict and the people involved.

1.  **Scenario:** A company creates an AI hiring tool that scans resumes to predict job performance. Later, it is discovered that the AI rejects resumes containing the names of women or graduates from all-women's colleges.
2.  Who are the stakeholders in this scenario? (List at least three groups affected by this technology).
3.  Explain *why* the AI made this unethical decision based on how it learns from data.

## Ethical Decision-Making Framework

Use a structured process to propose a solution and evaluate its impact on the community.

1.  Propose one specific action the company should take immediately to fix the problem identified in the scenario above.

## Reflection Questions

Consider your personal relationship with technology and the future of ethical AI.

1.  Do you believe AI should be held responsible for its mistakes, or should the human creators always be liable? Explain your answer in 2-3 sentences.
2.  How does learning about AI ethics change the way you view the apps and algorithms you use every day?

### Differentiation

*   **All Learners:** Complete the worksheet as written, working independently or in pairs to discuss the case study.
*   **Needs Support:** Provide a word bank for the definition section (e.g., "data," "mimic," "human," "automated") and sentence starters for the reflection questions (e.g., "I believe that...").
*   **Ready for More:** Research a real-world example of AI bias (such as in facial recognition or social media) and write a short paragraph comparing it to the case study in this worksheet.

### Answer Key

**Understanding AI Ethics**
1.  *Answers will vary, but should include concepts like machines performing tasks that usually require human intelligence.*
2.  *Examples may include: navigation apps, streaming recommendations, smart assistants, spam filters.*
3.  *Examples may include: privacy invasion, job loss, bias/discrimination, lack of human oversight.*

**Case Study Analysis**
1.  *Stakeholders: Job applicants (especially women), the company doing the hiring, the AI developers, current employees.*
2.  *The AI likely learned from historical hiring data that was dominated by men, teaching the algorithm to associate "success" with male characteristics or backgrounds.*

**Ethical Decision-Making Framework**
1.  *Answers will vary; might include: stop using the tool, audit the data for bias, retrain the model with diverse data, or remove gender-based indicators.*
2.  *Table answers depend on the solution chosen.*
3.  *Checklist examples: Did we test this on diverse groups? Does the training data represent all users? Is the decision-making process transparent?*

**Reflection Questions**
1.  *Answers will vary; look for reasoning regarding accountability and intent.*
2.  *Answers will vary; look for increased awareness of privacy, data usage, or potential bias.*
