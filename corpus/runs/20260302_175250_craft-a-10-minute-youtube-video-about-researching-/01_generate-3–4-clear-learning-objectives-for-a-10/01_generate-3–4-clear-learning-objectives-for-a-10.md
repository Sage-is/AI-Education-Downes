## Learning Objectives

**Course:** learning motivation research
**Audience:** general adults / aspiring educators
**Level:** Beginner

### Objectives
1. Distinguish between intrinsic and extrinsic motivation by creating a comparative chart that accurately identifies at least three distinct characteristics and examples of each type.
2. Summarize the core principles of Self-Determination Theory by drafting a short explanatory guide that correctly defines the roles of autonomy, competence, and relatedness in fostering learner engagement.
3. Apply the concept of growth mindset to a classroom scenario by designing a specific feedback script that praises effort and strategy use rather than innate ability.
4. Analyze a case study of a disengaged learner by identifying two potential motivational barriers and proposing one research-based intervention strategy for each identified barrier.
