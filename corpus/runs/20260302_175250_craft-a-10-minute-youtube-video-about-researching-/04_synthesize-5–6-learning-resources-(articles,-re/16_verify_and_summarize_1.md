### Summary
This University of Chicago library guide explains the SIFT method (Stop, Investigate the Source, Find Better Coverage, Trace Claims) developed by Mike Caulfield for evaluating online information credibility. The resource provides practical strategies for analyzing news, videos, and other digital media to determine reliability and detect misinformation.

### Key Concepts
SIFT method, lateral reading, source investigation, fact-checking, tracing claims to original context, misinformation evaluation, media literacy

### Relevance Assessment
The SIFT method directly addresses how to evaluate video and media claims by tracing them back to original research sources and verifying whether evidence supports the claims being made.

### Suggested Curriculum Use
Educators can use this framework to teach students a systematic approach for verifying whether video claims are supported by legitimate research, including how to trace citations and identify cherry-picked or out-of-context information.
