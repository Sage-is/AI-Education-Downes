# AI Literacy: Ethics of Plagiarism and Co-Creation - Syllabus

## Course Information

- **Duration:** 4 weeks
- **Modality:** In-person

## Course Modules

### Module 1: Defining Plagiarism and Co-Creation
Introduces beginner-level definitions of plagiarism and ethical co-creation in AI contexts, using scenarios to build recognition skills.  
- Define plagiarism and co-creation using accurate, beginner-level descriptions in the context of AI tools.  
- Identify examples of plagiarism versus ethical co-creation in AI usage scenarios.  
- Signature activities:  
  - Interactive group analysis of five AI scenarios with peer voting and discussion.  
  - Role-play skits demonstrating plagiarism vs. co-creation.  
- Formative assessment: Short multiple-choice quiz on scenario identification (100% accuracy target).

### Module 2: Ethical Harms and Classification
Examines why AI plagiarism harms creators and society, then practices classifying student work as plagiarized or ethically co-created.  
- Explain three ethical reasons why AI plagiarism harms creators and society.  
- Classify sample student assignments as plagiarized or ethically co-created with AI, justifying with evidence. 
- Signature activities:  
  - Small-group brainstorming of harms with real-world examples shared via whiteboard.  
  - Peer review of ten anonymized assignments with justification sticky notes.  
- Summative assessment: Written response classifying ten assignments with evidence.

### Module 3: Practicing Ethical Co-Creation
Guides hands-on creation of AI-co-created content with proper citation and analysis of real-world misuse cases.  
- Create a one-paragraph story co-created with an AI tool that includes a proper citation.  
- Analyze a real-world case study of AI misuse to determine ethical violations, supporting with reasons.  
- Signature activities:  
  - Paired AI collaboration stations for story drafting and citation practice.  
  - Jigsaw case study analysis with gallery walk presentations.  
- Formative assessment: Submitted AI-co-created story with citation.

### Module 4: Evaluating Disclosure and Personal Ethics
Explores pros/cons of AI disclosure through debate and culminates in developing a personal ethical pledge for AI use.  
- Evaluate pros and cons of disclosing AI co-creation in schoolwork during debate.  
- Develop a personal ethical pledge for AI use with at least five specific guidelines.  
- Signature activities:  
  - Structured class debate with prepared pros/cons notes.  
  - Personal pledge workshop with peer feedback circles.  
- Summative assessment: Personal ethical pledge presentation.
