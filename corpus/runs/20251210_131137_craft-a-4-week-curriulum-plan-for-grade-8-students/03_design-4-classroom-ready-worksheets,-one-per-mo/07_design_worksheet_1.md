# Worksheet: Module 4: Ethical AI Co-Creation Practices

- **Audience**: Grade 8 students
- **Skill focus**: Developing strategies for ethical use of AI in creative work without plagiarizing
- **Estimated time**: 30 minutes
- **Materials**: Pencil, notebook

## Warm-Up Prompt
Kick off by reflecting on your own experiences with AI to build awareness of ethical choices in creative projects.

1. Jot down one example of a time you used AI (like ChatGPT or an image generator) for something creative, such as writing a story or drawing.
2. Note what you did next: Did you copy it exactly, change it a lot, or add your own ideas? Why?
3. Circle one word that describes how that felt—fair, sneaky, exciting, or unsure?

## Guided Practice
Now, discover key strategies for co-creating ethically with AI, like a team where your ideas lead the way.

1. Read these rules: (1) Always credit AI use, (2) Transform AI output with your unique changes, (3) Use AI as a spark, not the final product. Pick one and explain in your notebook why it prevents plagiarism.
2. In the table below, sort these actions as "Ethical 👍" or "Not Ethical 👎":

| Action | Ethical? |
|--------|----------|
| Copy AI story word-for-word as your homework |          |
| Ask AI for story ideas, then rewrite in your voice |          |
| Submit AI-generated art without changes |          |

3. Add one more "Ethical 👍" action of your own.

## Apply & Create
Put it all together by designing your personal guideline—your roadmap for awesome, honest AI teamwork!

1. Brainstorm 3-5 rules for yourself, like "I will always add my twist" or "I'll tell my teacher when AI helped."
2. Write them as a numbered list titled "My Ethical AI Co-Creation Pledge."
3. Draw a quick symbol (like a handshake) next to your favorite rule to make it memorable.

## Reflection & Exit Ticket
Wrap up by thinking about how these practices empower your creativity— you've got this!

1. How will your personal guideline change the way you use AI next time? Write 2 sentences.
2. Exit ticket: On a scale of 1-5 (5 = super confident), rate your readiness to co-create ethically with AI. Explain why in one sentence.

## Differentiation
- **All Learners**: Follow tasks as written, using notebook space for responses.
- **Needs Support**: Pair with a buddy for brainstorming in Apply & Create, and use sentence starters like "My rule is..." for the pledge.
- **Ready for More**: Extend Apply & Create by sharing your pledge with a partner and role-playing an ethical AI scenario together.

## Answer Key
- **Warm-Up Prompt**: Ex. 1: Used AI for poem ideas. Ex. 2: Changed words to make it mine—felt fair. Ex. 3: Exciting (variations accepted).
- **Guided Practice**: 1: Ex. "Transforming adds my originality, so it's not copying." 2: Copy = 👎; Rewrite = 👍; Submit unchanged = 👎. 3: Ex. "Mix AI with my drawings."
- **Apply & Create**: Ex. pledge: 1. Credit AI. 2. Change 50%+. 3. Ideas only, not full work. Symbol: thumbs up.
- **Reflection & Exit Ticket**: Ex. "I'll feel proud knowing it's truly mine. 4/5—need practice but excited!" (personal responses summarized; focus on ethical intent).
