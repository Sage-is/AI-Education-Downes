# Worksheet: Module 3: Principles of Co-Creation with AI

- **Audience:** Grade 8 students
- **Skill Focus:** Distinguishing between plagiarism and ethical co-creation using AI
- **Estimated Time:** 30 minutes
- **Materials:** Pencil, notebook

## Warm-Up Prompt
Kick off by reflecting on your experiences with AI tools to build awareness of ethical use.

1. Jot down one way you've used (or could use) an AI tool like ChatGPT for homework, such as brainstorming ideas or writing a paragraph.
2. Did you copy the AI output exactly, tweak it with your own words, or start fresh? Explain why in 1-2 sentences.
3. Share one idea with a partner—what makes it "yours"?

## Guided Practice
Explore the key principles of co-creation: collaborate with AI as a partner, always add your unique voice, and credit the tool.

1. In your notebook, define plagiarism (using someone else's work as your own without credit) and ethical co-creation (working with AI to enhance your ideas while making it original).
2. Review this table and mark "Plagiarism" or "Co-Creation" for each scenario:

| Scenario | Type |
|----------|------|
| Copy-pasting an AI-generated essay without changes |      |
| Asking AI for story ideas, then rewriting in your style and citing "AI helped brainstorm" |      |
| Submitting AI's full poem as yours |      |

3. Discuss with a partner: What's one principle that stands out?

## Apply & Create
Put your skills to the test by analyzing examples and creating your own ethical co-creation.

1. Read these two project responses to the prompt "Write a paragraph on recycling":  
   - Response A: Exact copy from AI.  
   - Response B: AI suggested facts; student added personal story and cited AI.  
   Which is ethical co-creation? Why?
2. Create your own: Use an AI tool (or imagine one) to get 3 bullet points on "benefits of teamwork." Rewrite them into a short original paragraph with your twist.
3. Label it: Does your paragraph follow co-creation principles? Check yes/no and explain.

## Reflection & Exit Ticket
Wrap up by solidifying what ethical co-creation means for you moving forward.

1. In 2-3 sentences, explain one key principle of co-creation with AI and how it differs from plagiarism.
2. Exit Ticket: On a sticky note, write "I will co-create ethically by..." (one action you'll take).

## Differentiation
- **All Learners:** Follow tasks as written, using partner discussions for support.
- **Needs Support:** For Apply & Create #1, provide printed scenario cards and work with a buddy to label examples verbally first.
- **Ready for More:** In Apply & Create #2, co-create a full infographic outline with AI, then debate ethics with the class.

## Answer Key
- **Warm-Up Prompt:** 1. Examples: AI for essay outlines, math help. 2. Ethical if tweaked/original; plagiarism if copied. 3. Builds ownership.
- **Guided Practice:** 1. Plagiarism: No credit/changes; Co-creation: Credit + originality. 2. Table: Plagiarism, Co-Creation, Plagiarism. 3. E.g., "Add your voice."
- **Apply & Create:** 1. B is co-creation (original + credit). 2. Student paragraph shows changes/credit. 3. Yes if original/credited.
- **Reflection & Exit Ticket:** 1. E.g., "Co-creation means partnering with AI, adding my ideas and crediting it—unlike plagiarism's copying." 2. E.g., "Citing AI and rewriting."
