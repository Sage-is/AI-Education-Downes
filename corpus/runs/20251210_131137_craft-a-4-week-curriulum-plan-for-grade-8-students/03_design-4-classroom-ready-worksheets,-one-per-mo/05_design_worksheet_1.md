# Worksheet: Module 2: Detecting Plagiarism in AI Outputs

**Overview**
- **Audience:** Grade 8 students
- **Skill Focus:** Analyzing text to detect if it was generated or plagiarized by AI
- **Estimated Time:** 30 minutes
- **Materials:** Pencil, notebook

## Warm-Up Prompt
Kick off by brainstorming what plagiarism means today with AI tools—get ready to spot sneaky copies!

1. In your notebook, jot down 2-3 signs of plagiarism in human writing (like exact copied sentences).
2. How might AI make plagiarism harder to detect? List 1-2 ideas, like "perfect grammar but no personality."
3. Share one idea with a partner—what do you both think?

## Guided Practice
Practice your detective skills on these sample paragraphs about climate change—one is student-written, one is AI-generated plagiarism.

**Sample A:** "Climate change is bad because the Earth is getting hotter. People drive cars too much and factories pollute. We should use bikes and recycle. Governments need to make laws."

**Sample B:** "Last summer, my family’s beach trip got rained out every day, even though forecasts said sunny. I read how melting ice caps cause weird weather, so now I turn off lights and walk to school."

1. Circle 2 clues in Sample A that scream "AI-generated" (hint: generic facts, no personal touch).
2. Underline 1 clue in Sample B that feels human-written.
3. Rate each: Plagiarized by AI? (Yes/No) and explain why in 1 sentence.

## Apply & Create
Now you're the expert—analyze new texts and build your own detection tool!

**Sample C:** "Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert sunlight into energy using chlorophyll. This vital mechanism sustains life on Earth."

**Sample D:** "Photosynthesis helps plants make food from sun and air. In science class, we grew beans under different lights— the ones in dark barely grew!"

1. Decide: Is Sample C or D more likely AI-plagiarized? Mark it and list 2 reasons.
2. Create a 4-item checklist for spotting AI outputs (e.g., "Repeats boring phrases?").

## Reflection & Exit Ticket
Reflect on your new skills—you're now a plagiarism pro!

1. What’s your top detection strategy from today?
2. How will you use this in your own writing? (1 sentence)
3. Exit Ticket: Rate your confidence spotting AI plagiarism (1-5) and why.

## Differentiation
- **All Learners:** Follow tasks as written with partner support for sharing.
- **Needs Support:** Provide printed Sample texts with clues pre-highlighted; simplify checklist to 3 items.
- **Ready for More:** Analyze a partner’s writing sample or research one real AI detector tool online.

## Answer Key
**Warm-Up Prompt:** 1. Signs like matching words/phrases from sources. 2. AI creates "original" remixes that sound good but lack voice. 3. Open share.

**Guided Practice:** 1. Sample A: generic facts, repetitive structure. 2. Sample B: personal story. 3. A=Yes (bland, no specifics); B=No (personal experience).

**Apply & Create:** 1. C=Yes (textbook copy, no personality); D=No (class example). 2. Checklists should include generic phrasing, lack of details, overly formal tone, etc.

**Reflection & Exit Ticket:** Open responses; look for strategy mentions (e.g., "personal stories") and confidence growth.
