	# 4-Week Curriculum Plan: AI Literacy - Ethics of Plagiarism and Co-Creation

## Scope Summary
This beginner-level, in-person curriculum for grade 8 students (ages 13-14) spans 4 weeks (6 hours/week), building foundational AI literacy through definitions, identification, ethical analysis, hands-on co-creation, and personal pledges. It emphasizes distinguishing plagiarism from ethical co-creation, using interactive activities, worksheets, debates, and real-world scenarios to foster responsible AI use.

## Learning Objectives
1. Define plagiarism and co-creation using accurate, beginner-level descriptions in the context of AI tools.
2. Identify examples of plagiarism versus ethical co-creation in five provided AI usage scenarios with 100% accuracy.
3. Explain three ethical reasons why AI plagiarism harms creators and society in a short written response.
4. Classify ten sample student assignments as plagiarized or ethically co-created with AI, justifying each with evidence.
5. Create a one-paragraph story co-created with an AI tool that includes a proper citation of the AI's contribution.
6. Analyze a real-world case study of AI misuse to determine if it violates ethical standards, supporting with two key reasons.
7. Evaluate the pros and cons of disclosing AI co-creation in schoolwork during a class debate using prepared notes.
8. Develop a personal ethical pledge for AI use containing at least five specific, actionable guidelines.

## Module Outline
Each module aligns to 1-2 objectives, includes signature activities, and a dedicated worksheet (30 minutes each).

### Module 1: Defining Plagiarism and Co-Creation
- Focus: Core definitions and scenario recognition.
- Activities: Group analysis of five AI scenarios; role-play skits.
- Worksheet: [Module 1: Understanding Plagiarism in AI Contexts](https://example.com/worksheet1) – Defines terms, identifies examples.
- Formative: Multiple-choice quiz (target: 100% accuracy).

### Module 2: Ethical Harms and Classification
- Focus: Consequences and classification skills.
- Activities: Brainstorm harms; peer review of ten assignments.
- Worksheet: [Module 2: Detecting Plagiarism in AI Outputs](https://example.com/worksheet2) – Analyzes texts for AI indicators.
- Summative: Written classification of ten assignments with evidence.

### Module 3: Practicing Ethical Co-Creation
- Focus: Hands-on creation and case analysis.
- Activities: Paired AI story drafting; jigsaw case studies.
- Worksheet: [Module 3: Principles of Co-Creation with AI](https://example.com/worksheet3) – Distinguishes practices via scenarios.
- Formative: Submitted AI-co-created story with citation.

### Module 4: Evaluating Disclosure and Personal Ethics
- Focus: Debate and personal commitment.
- Activities: Class debate on disclosure; pledge workshop.
- Worksheet: [Module 4: Ethical AI Co-Creation Practices](https://example.com/worksheet4) – Builds personal guidelines.
- Summative: Ethical pledge presentation.

## Assessments
Assessments blend formative (quizzes, worksheets) and summative (projects, presentations) with rubrics scaled: Beginning, Developing, Proficient, Advanced.

| Assessment Type | Aligned Objectives | Description | Rubric Highlights |
|-----------------|--------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Quiz (Formative)** | 1-2 | 6-8 scenarios; classify with justifications (90% accuracy target). | Proficient: 75-89% accuracy, consistent justifications. |
| **Written Response (Summative)** | 3-4 | Paragraph on harms; classify 10 assignments. | Proficient: Three reasons; evidence-based classifications. |
| **Project: AI-Co-Created Story (Formative)** | 5 | One-paragraph story with citation. | Proficient: Original, properly attributed. |
| **Case Analysis (Formative)** | 6 | Real-world misuse evaluation. | Proficient: Two supported reasons. |
| **Debate (Formative)** | 7 | Pros/cons of disclosure. | Proficient: Two arguments/side, coherent delivery. |
| **Personal Pledge Presentation (Summative)** | 8 | Five guidelines with rationale. | Proficient: Actionable, justified rules. |

**Grading Criteria Template**:

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Accuracy/Correctness | 40% |
| Clarity/Communication | 30% |
| Application/Transfer | 30% |
