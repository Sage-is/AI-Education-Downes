## Learning Objectives

**Course:** AI Literacy: Ethics of Plagiarism and Co-Creation
**Audience:** grade 8 students
**Level:** Beginner

### Objectives
1. Define plagiarism and ethical co-creation using AI tools with 100% accuracy in a graphic organizer.
2. Identify examples of plagiarism versus proper co-creation in AI-generated text samples with at least 90% accuracy.
3. Explain the ethical consequences of AI plagiarism in a short written paragraph using at least three key reasons.
4. Classify ten provided scenarios involving AI use as ethical or unethical plagiarism with 85% accuracy.
5. Create an original story paragraph co-created with AI that includes proper attribution and avoids plagiarism.
6. Evaluate a peer's AI-assisted artwork for ethical co-creation standards using a provided rubric scoring at least 80%.
7. Debate the benefits and risks of AI co-creation in school assignments, articulating two arguments per side coherently.
