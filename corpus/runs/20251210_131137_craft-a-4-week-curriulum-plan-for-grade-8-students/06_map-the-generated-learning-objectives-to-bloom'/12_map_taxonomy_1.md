## Bloom's Taxonomy Mapping

| Objective | Level | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| 1. Identify at least three ethical dilemmas in a real-world AI application case study, such as facial recognition, with supporting evidence from the scenario. | **Analyze** | Requires breaking down the case study to distinguish dilemmas and connect evidence. |
| 2. Explain how algorithmic bias impacts decision-making in AI systems using a provided example dataset, demonstrating cause-and-effect relationships accurately. | **Understand** | Involves explaining concepts and relationships using an example. |
| 3. Analyze the privacy risks of AI data collection in social media apps and propose two mitigation strategies with justifications. | **Evaluate** | Includes judging risks and justifying proposed strategies. |
| 4. Evaluate the fairness of an AI recommendation algorithm by testing it on diverse user profiles and scoring outcomes on a 1-5 equity rubric. | **Evaluate** | Directly involves assessing and scoring fairness using criteria. |
| 5. Create a digital infographic outlining five key ethical principles for responsible AI use in everyday life, incorporating accurate references to course concepts. | **Create** | Requires producing an original digital artifact synthesizing concepts. |
