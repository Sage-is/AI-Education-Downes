## Learning Objectives

**Course:** AI Literacy Ethics
**Audience:** grade 8 students
**Level:** Intermediate

### Objectives
1. Identify at least three ethical dilemmas in a real-world AI application case study, such as facial recognition, with supporting evidence from the scenario.
2. Explain how algorithmic bias impacts decision-making in AI systems using a provided example dataset, demonstrating cause-and-effect relationships accurately.
3. Analyze the privacy risks of AI data collection in social media apps and propose two mitigation strategies with justifications.
4. Evaluate the fairness of an AI recommendation algorithm by testing it on diverse user profiles and scoring outcomes on a 1-5 equity rubric.
5. Create a digital infographic outlining five key ethical principles for responsible AI use in everyday life, incorporating accurate references to course concepts.
