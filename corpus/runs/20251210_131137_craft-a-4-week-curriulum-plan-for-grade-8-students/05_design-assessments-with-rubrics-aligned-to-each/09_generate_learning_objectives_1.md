## Learning Objectives

**Course:** AI literacy: ethics of plagiarism and co-creation
**Audience:** grade 8 students
**Level:** Beginner

### Objectives
1. Define plagiarism and co-creation in the context of AI-generated content, articulating accurate distinctions in a short paragraph.
2. Identify examples of plagiarism versus ethical co-creation in sample AI-assisted texts, classifying at least five instances with 90% accuracy.
3. Explain the ethical risks of unattributed AI use, referencing two real-world scenarios in a brief oral presentation.
4. Differentiate acceptable AI co-creation from plagiarism in a given writing task, producing a revised artifact with proper attribution.
5. Develop a personal ethical guideline for AI collaboration, outlining three rules with justifications for beginner-level application.
