## Pacing Guide

**Total Duration:** 4 weeks
**Modules:** 4
**Hours Per Week:** 6

### Weekly Schedule

| Week | Module | Total Hours | Content | Practice | Assessment | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 6h | 2.4h | 2.4h | 1.2h | Core concepts + practice |
| 2 | 2 | 6h | 2.4h | 2.4h | 1.2h | Core concepts + practice |
| 3 | 3 | 6h | 2.4h | 2.4h | 1.2h | Core concepts + practice |
| 4 | 4 | 6h | 2.4h | 2.4h | 1.2h | Core concepts + practice |

### Weekly Highlights

- **Week 1 (Module 1):** Introduce core concepts and foundational skills through readings, videos, and interactive exercises; checkpoint: Module 1 quiz (6 hours).
- **Week 2 (Module 2):** Dive into practical applications and hands-on practice with guided tutorials; checkpoint: Signature activity - first project submission (6 hours).
- **Week 3 (Module 3):** Build advanced techniques via case studies and peer discussions; checkpoint: Mid-course reflection and peer review (6 hours).
- **Week 4 (Module 4):** Synthesize learning in a capstone project with real-world application; checkpoint: Final portfolio submission and course evaluation (6 hours).
