### Summary
Sage.is AI-UI is an open-source, privacy-focused AI interface platform designed for startups, small teams, and schools that allows users to run AI models on their own infrastructure. The platform supports multiple AI providers (Ollama, OpenAI, Anthropic), features RAG-powered knowledge bases, team collaboration tools, and a Community Hub for sharing models and prompts. It is licensed under AGPL v3 and offers enterprise features like SSO and detailed audit logs.

### Key Concepts
Privacy-first AI deployment, multi-model chat interface, RAG knowledge bases, self-hosted infrastructure, team permissions and access control, community hub for sharing resources, plugin architecture and extensibility
