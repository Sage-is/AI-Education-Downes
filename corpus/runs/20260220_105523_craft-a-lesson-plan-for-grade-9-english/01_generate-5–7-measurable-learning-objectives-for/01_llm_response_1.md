# Measurable Learning Objectives — Grade 9 English

Here are 7 measurable learning objectives using action verbs aligned with **Bloom's Taxonomy**, appropriate for high school level:

---

### Knowledge & Comprehension
1. **Identify** the key literary devices (e.g., metaphor, simile, personification, imagery) used in a given text with at least 80% accuracy.

2. **Summarize** the central theme and supporting details of a short story or informational text in a well-organized paragraph of 5–7 sentences.

### Application & Analysis
3. **Analyze** how an author's word choice and tone contribute to the overall mood of a passage, citing at least three specific textual examples as evidence.

4. **Compare and contrast** two characters' motivations and development within a literary work using a structured graphic organizer or essay format.

### Synthesis & Evaluation
5. **Construct** a well-structured argumentative paragraph that includes a clear claim, relevant textual evidence, and logical reasoning to support a position on a theme from the text.

6. **Evaluate** the effectiveness of an author's use of narrative perspective (first-person vs. third-person) by composing a written response that defends a judgment with at least two pieces of supporting evidence.

7. **Create** an original short narrative (150–250 words) that deliberately incorporates at least three literary devices studied in the unit, demonstrating purposeful craft choices.

---

### Design Principles Applied

| Principle | How It's Reflected |
|---|---|
| **Measurability** | Each objective includes observable actions and specific criteria (e.g., "at least three examples," "80% accuracy," "5–7 sentences") |
| **Action Verbs** | Verbs drawn from Bloom's Taxonomy: *identify, summarize, analyze, compare/contrast, construct, evaluate, create* |
| **Progression** | Objectives move from lower-order thinking (identify, summarize) to higher-order thinking (evaluate, create) |
| **Grade Appropriateness** | Complexity, text types, and output expectations are calibrated for Grade 9 learners |
| **Specificity** | Each objective clarifies *what* the student will do, *how* they will demonstrate it, and the *standard* for success |

These objectives can be directly embedded into a lesson plan and used to design aligned assessments and activities.
