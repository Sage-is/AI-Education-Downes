# Pacing Guide: Grade 9 English Lesson Plan

## Overview

| Component | % of Total Time | Purpose |
|-----------|:-:|---------|
| Content Delivery | 30% | Direct instruction, modeling, lectures, reading |
| Practice | 50% | Guided practice, independent work, collaborative activities |
| Assessment | 20% | Formative checks, summative tasks, reflection |

## Key Pacing Principles

1. **Gradual Release Model** — Each module moves from heavier content delivery early on to predominantly practice and assessment by the final weeks.
2. **Practice-Heavy Design** — Over half of instructional time is dedicated to student practice, reflecting research on active learning in ELA.
3. **Ongoing Formative Assessment** — Low-stakes checks (exit tickets, journals, peer feedback) are embedded daily so instruction can be adjusted in real time.
4. **Summative Anchors** — Each module concludes with a major assessment tied to a specific writing mode or skill set.
5. **Flex Days** — Build in 1-2 flex days per module (not shown) for re-teaching, enrichment, or catch-up based on formative data.

---

This pacing guide assumes **~45-50 minute class periods, 5 days per week, across a 27-week instructional year** (with remaining weeks reserved for school events, testing windows, and transitions).
