### **Part 4: Argumentative Writing (20 points)**
*Learning Objectives: Write arguments to support claims (W.9.1), Use evidence from texts (W.9.1b)*

**Prompt:** Should schools require students to read a set list of classic novels, or should students be allowed to choose their own reading material? 

Write a **well-organized argumentative paragraph** (8–10 sentences) that includes:

- [ ] A clear **claim** (your position)
- [ ] At least **two** pieces of supporting evidence or reasoning
- [ ] A **counterclaim** and your response to it
- [ ] A **concluding statement** that reinforces your position
