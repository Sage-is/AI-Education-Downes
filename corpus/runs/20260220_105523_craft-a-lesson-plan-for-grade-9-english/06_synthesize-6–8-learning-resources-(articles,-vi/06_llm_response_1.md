## Grade 9 English Language Arts — Curated Learning Resources

### Articles & Reading Materials

**1. ReadWriteThink — Lesson Plans & Student Materials**
- **Source:** ReadWriteThink.org (IRA/NCTE — .org, widely recognized OER)
- **Type:** Interactive lessons, articles, printables
- **Focus:** Literary analysis, persuasive writing, close reading strategies
- **Why it fits:** Standards-aligned for Grades 9–10 ELA; peer-reviewed by educators

**2. CK-12 — English Language Arts FlexBooks**
- **Source:** CK-12.org (OER Foundation)
- **Type:** Open textbook / digital reading
- **Focus:** Grammar, composition, literary genres, vocabulary building
- **Why it fits:** Fully free, customizable, aligned to Common Core ELA standards

**3. MIT OpenCourseWare — Introduction to Literature (Adapted)**
- **Source:** ocw.mit.edu (.edu)
- **Type:** Articles, lecture notes, reading lists
- **Focus:** Poetry, short fiction, drama analysis — adaptable for advanced 9th graders
- **Why it fits:** Rigorous .edu source; reading materials can scaffold critical thinking

### Video Resources

**4. Khan Academy — Grammar & Reading Comprehension**
- **Source:** KhanAcademy.org (OER / free platform)
- **Type:** Video lessons + practice exercises
- **Focus:** Grammar conventions, reading informational text, SAT-aligned reading prep
- **Why it fits:** Self-paced, standards-aligned, includes embedded assessments

**5. Crash Course Literature (YouTube / PBS)**
- **Source:** YouTube.com/CrashCourse (created in partnership with PBS Digital Studios)
- **Type:** Video series
- **Focus:** Classic novels commonly taught in Grade 9 (*To Kill a Mockingbird*, *Romeo and Juliet*, *The Great Gatsby*)
- **Why it fits:** Engaging, high-production-value summaries and analysis; great for flipped classroom use

### OER Curriculum Materials

**6. OER Commons — Grade 9 ELA Collections**
- **Source:** OERCommons.org (OER)
- **Type:** Full lesson plans, rubrics, curated resource sets
- **Focus:** Argumentative writing, literary analysis, informational reading
- **Why it fits:** Peer-reviewed, CC-licensed, searchable by standard and grade level

**7. EngageNY / New York State Education — Grade 9 ELA Modules**
- **Source:** engageny.org (supported by NYSED — state .gov/.edu aligned)
- **Type:** Complete curriculum modules (units, assessments, texts)
- **Focus:** Close reading of complex texts, evidence-based writing, academic vocabulary
- **Why it fits:** Full, free, field-tested curriculum; aligned to CCSS ELA

**8. Purdue OWL — Writing Resources for High School**
- **Source:** owl.purdue.edu (.edu)
- **Type:** Articles, guides, exercises
- **Focus:** Essay structure, MLA/APA citation, grammar mechanics, research writing
- **Why it fits:** Authoritative .edu writing reference; widely used across secondary and post-secondary education
